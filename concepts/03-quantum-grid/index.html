    07 / ABOUT

    Founded by people who had to run it afterwards.

    TECHBISS was started in 2011 by four platform engineers who kept being handed transformation programmes designed by people who would never be paged at four in the morning. We built the company we wished had shown up.

    The thesis

    Enterprise change fails for an unglamorous reason: nobody can see the whole system at once, so decisions are made on fragments. We built an instrument that resolves an entire operating estate — infrastructure, data, applications, cost and people — onto one addressable grid, and we refuse to move anything that has not been resolved onto it first.

    Fifteen years later that principle is still the whole company. It is why we publish rollback ceilings, why our contracts contain exit plans, and why our headcount on an account is designed to fall rather than grow. We would rather be re-hired than retained.
