TTECHBISS Design directions · 2026

Round one — five directions

Five ways TECHBISS could look in 2026.

Each one is a complete, working homepage — not a picture of a homepage. Real motion, real interaction, real copy from the business: one partner for domain, hosting, SSL, website, apps, email, SEO and support. Open them, scroll them, hover things. Then pick the one that feels like the company, and that becomes the full site.

01

Aurora Glass — quiet luxury

Near-black canvas with a live aurora field drifting behind frosted glass panels. A custom cursor that swells over anything clickable, buttons that lean toward the pointer, and a bento grid whose cards tilt in 3D and light up under a spotlight.

Choose this ifYou want TECHBISS to read as expensive and calm — the agency other agencies hire. Ages well; nothing here is a trend.
  • Aurora canvas
  • Magnetic cursor
  • 3D tilt bento
  • Scroll-linked process
  • Price toggle

02

Kinetic Editorial — confident, light

Warm paper stock, enormous type set the way a magazine sets it, and a services rail you drag sideways. Text scrambles into place as it enters view, a labelled cursor tells you what a link does, and the whole page reads like a printed annual report that happens to move.

Choose this ifYour clients are business owners, not developers. Light backgrounds convert better with non-technical buyers and print beautifully in a proposal.
  • Drag rail
  • Text scramble
  • Labelled cursor
  • Accordion process
  • Reading progress

03

Signal Terminal — proof, not promises

A control room. A console streams live operations lines as you read, sparklines redraw themselves, a wireframe globe turns with client locations pulsing on it, and every service sits in a status table marked LIVE / VALID / DELIVERING.

Choose this ifYour strongest sales argument is that you keep things running after launch. This design argues it visually before anyone reads a word.
  • Live console
  • Canvas globe
  • Sparklines
  • Status matrix
  • Typewriter hero

04

Liquid Chrome — loud and modern

Metallic display type whose shimmer tracks your scroll, coloured light pooling behind the page, and four service blocks that stack on top of one another as you move — each one dimming and shrinking as the next slides over it.

Choose this ifYou want to look like the newest studio in the market. Highest visual impact of the five, and the most memorable on a phone.

  • Stacking cards
  • Metaball light

05

Blueprint Stack — explain the offer

Deep navy drafting paper. The hero holds an isometric diagram that draws itself layer by layer — domain, hosting, SSL, email, website — with packets travelling up the spine, and the process section builds a glowing timeline as you scroll past it.

Choose this ifThe hardest part of your sale is explaining what "full web setup" actually includes. This design makes the stack visible in one glance.
