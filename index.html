How a project goes live.

Four steps, the same order every time — nothing skipped to hit a date.

01

Talk

No sales script — you describe what the business runs on today and what's missing from it. We come back with a written scope, a fixed price and a date, before anyone commits to anything.

02

Build

Domain, hosting, SSL, email, the website and whatever apps the business actually needs — set up as one system, not six. You see a working preview link, not a slide deck, and you review it as it grows.

03

Launch

Live, checked on real devices, indexed, and handed over with every login and record documented in your name — not dropped and forgotten. We walk your team through editing it themselves.

04

Watch

Renewals, SSL, hosting, backups and uptime kept ahead of their dates, so nothing lapses quietly. When something needs changing, you message one person and it gets done.
