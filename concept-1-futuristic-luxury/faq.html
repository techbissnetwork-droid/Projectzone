FAQ

Answers, before you even ask.

Common questions about working with TECHBISS — pricing, process, support and security.

General

TECHBISS is a complete digital-transformation partner. We design and build websites, web and mobile applications, and handle the infrastructure behind them — domains, hosting, SSL, security and business email — so you have one partner instead of five vendors.

Yes. Business digitization — turning an offline operation into a complete digital business — is a core part of what we do, from the first website to the systems that run behind it.

We work across retail, healthcare, finance, hospitality, logistics, education and professional services. Our process adapts to your industry, not the other way around.

In most cases, yes. We start with a technical and design audit before proposing the best path forward, whether that's improving what exists or rebuilding it.

Pricing & Quotes

Starter and Business plans have transparent starting prices. Professional and Enterprise projects are scoped individually and receive a custom quotation based on complexity, timeline and integrations.

Custom systems, integrations and infrastructure vary significantly project to project. A custom quotation ensures the price reflects the actual scope — not a generic package.

No. Every proposal outlines what's included, what's optional, and what ongoing costs (like hosting or support plans) look like before work begins.

Yes. Projects are typically billed across agreed milestones tied to project phases, not a single upfront payment.

Process & Timelines

A standard website is usually delivered in 3–6 weeks. Web and mobile applications and full digitization programs typically run 8–16 weeks depending on scope.

Discover, Strategy, Design, Develop, Test, Launch and Grow — each phase has clear deliverables and check-ins, so you always know what stage your project is in.

Yes. We share progress at each phase and review key milestones together rather than disappearing until launch.

Launch is a phase, not an end point. Every plan includes a support window, and ongoing growth, maintenance and optimization can continue under a support plan.

Support & Security

Support ranges from bug fixes and updates to full managed hosting, monitoring and continuous improvement, depending on your plan.

Every project includes baseline security practices, and SSL, HTTPS and hardened configurations are standard — not an add-on — across everything we build.

Yes. We offer managed domain, hosting and infrastructure services so your systems stay fast, secure and online.

Support plans include defined response times. Enterprise clients receive 24/7 priority response for critical issues.
