Migrating? No Problem

Moving from your current host doesn't mean going offline.

We've moved businesses off hosts that were actively failing them, without a single dropped order or bounced email along the way. Here's how.

  • Files, database and DNS copied over before anything live is touched
  • Your current site stays online until the new one is fully verified
  • Email keeps flowing throughout — nothing gets dropped mid-move
  • One switchover, timed for your lowest-traffic hours
What "no downtime" actually means

Your current site stays live the entire time.
