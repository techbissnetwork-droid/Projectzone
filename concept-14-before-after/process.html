Process

From your first yes to a system that runs itself — here's exactly what happens.

No black box, no disappearing for six weeks. Four stages, each with real deliverables and a real timeline — from the day you sign off to the day you're live, and every step in between.

Before: a vague promise of "a few weeks." After: four stages you can actually see.
How we work

Four steps, start to finish.

The same four stages as the rest of the site — Discover, Design & Build, Launch, Grow — laid out here with what actually happens, what you get, and how long each one takes.

Discover

Typically 1–2 weeks

Before we design or build anything, we get specific about what's actually slowing your business down — and where fixing it pays off fastest. That means real conversations with the people who use the current process every day, not just whoever signs the contract.

  • A written scope document you sign off on before design starts
  • A map of your current workflow — what to keep, what to replace
  • A recommended starting point from our Pricing packages
  • A fixed timeline and price, agreed before work begins
Before: we think something's wrong, we're just not sure what. After: a written plan everyone agrees on.

Design & Build

Typically 3–6 weeks

This is where the scope document becomes something you can actually click through. Our team designs and builds in short, visible sprints — you're never waiting six weeks to see the first result, and nothing goes into development you haven't already approved.

  • Design previews you review and sign off before development starts
  • Working builds on a private staging link, updated as we go
  • Scheduled check-ins at each milestone, not surprise silence
  • Content and data migration, if you're moving off an existing system
Before: imagining what the finished product feels like. After: a working preview you can click through yourself.

Launch

Typically 3–5 days

Launch day is boring, on purpose. By the time we go live, everything has already been tested against real data on the staging environment — domain, hosting, SSL and business email are configured, monitored and ready before your customers ever see the new system.

  • Domain connection, hosting and SSL configured and verified
  • Business email accounts set up and tested
  • A final walkthrough and training session with your team
  • Post-launch monitoring for the first two weeks, included
Before: crossing your fingers on launch day. After: a tested system and a team already watching it.

Grow

Ongoing

A launch is a starting line, not a finish line. We stay on to monitor uptime, ship small improvements, and extend the system as your business changes — so the thing we built together keeps getting more useful instead of quietly going stale.

  • Uptime and performance monitoring
  • A direct line to our team for questions and small changes
  • Optional ongoing support & maintenance retainer
  • Planning conversations as your business grows into new needs
Before: hoping someone notices when something breaks. After: monitoring that notices first.
How we communicate

You'll never have to wonder what's happening.

Here's exactly how we keep you in the loop — no black box, no vanishing act between kickoff and launch.

A shared project channel

Every project runs in a shared channel from kickoff to launch. Ask a question, get an answer the same business day — not a ticket number.

Staging links, not status PDFs

You see the actual work in progress on a private staging link, updated as we build — not a status report once a month.

Fixed scope, fixed price

The price and timeline we agree on at kickoff are the price and timeline you get. If something changes the scope, you hear about it before it changes the invoice.
