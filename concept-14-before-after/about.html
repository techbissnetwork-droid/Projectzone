About TECHBISS

We've done this before. Seventy times, actually.

TECHBISS exists to close one gap: the distance between businesses still running on paper and phone calls, and the ones already running on software that works without them in the room.

9 years building digital systems 70+ businesses moved from analog to digital 150+ digital products shipped
Why we exist

The businesses that switch, and the ones that wait.

Most small and mid-sized businesses aren't behind because they lack ambition. They're behind because the tools running their day-to-day were never built to grow with them — a booking calendar taped to the wall works fine at ten customers a week, and quietly falls apart at fifty. By the time anyone notices, the business has usually been losing orders, hours and trust for months.

We started TECHBISS to sit in exactly that gap. Not as a marketing agency that makes things look nicer, and not as a software vendor that hands over a tool and disappears — but as the team that rebuilds the actual plumbing. The ordering system. The booking flow. The inventory count. The invoice. We replace it with something that runs itself, updates itself, and tells the truth about what's happening right now.

Nine years and more than 150 shipped systems later, the pattern hasn't changed much. A business comes to us running on paper, spreadsheets, or a phone that never stops ringing. It leaves with a live system its whole team can see — and an owner who's stopped being the only person who knows what's actually going on.

Principles

Four rules we don't bend on.

Not a values poster on a wall. Just the four things that actually shape how a project with us goes.

01

We ship in weeks, not quarters.

A scoped project should be live in six to ten weeks, not stuck on a roadmap for a year. We work in short, visible sprints, so you're looking at something real within days.

Before: a nine-month agency timeline. After: a live link in week one.
02

We build what you'll actually use.

No feature you didn't ask for, no dashboard nobody opens. We design around the handful of things your team does every single day.

Before: software nobody logs into. After: the first tab open every morning.
03

We stay after launch.

Launch day is the start of the relationship, not the end of the invoice. We monitor, patch and extend what we build for as long as you're running it.

Before: a developer who goes dark after the invoice clears. After: a team that still picks up in year three.
04

We show the math, not just the mockup.

Every system we build reports on itself — orders, uptime, response time — so you're never left guessing whether it's actually working.

Before: "I think it's working?" After: a number on a dashboard that proves it.
