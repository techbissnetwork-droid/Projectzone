What Gets Digitized

Every part of the operation that's still running on paper, memory or luck.

Most businesses need two or three of these, not all six. Here's the full scope of what's possible once the operational core is digitized.

01

Online Ordering & Booking

Customers order or book directly, any hour, without waiting for someone to pick up a phone.

Before: orders taken over the phone, one at a time. After: customers order or book themselves, any hour.
02

Inventory & Stock Tracking

Stock levels update the moment something sells, across every location that carries it.

Before: a stock count on a clipboard, done once a week. After: stock levels that update the second something sells.
03

Payments

Cards, transfers and invoices are accepted and reconciled without anyone touching a spreadsheet.

Before: cash, cheques and an honesty jar. After: cards and transfers, reconciled automatically.
04

Customer Records & CRM

Every customer's history is searchable by anyone on the team, not stored in one person's memory.

Before: customer history living in one person's head. After: every customer's history, searchable in seconds.

Reporting & Alerts

Numbers that used to take an afternoon to pull together are already waiting for you each morning.

Before: month-end numbers pulled together by hand. After: dashboards and alerts that flag problems before you ask.
06

Rosters and shift swaps live in one place everyone can see, instead of a scattered group chat.

Before: a shift schedule texted out, group chat by group chat. After: a roster everyone can check themselves.
How It Usually Starts

You don't need to digitize everything at once.

That's the biggest misconception. Most businesses start with the one thing costing them the most time — a phone that rings all day, or a stock count that eats a Sunday — get that live, and decide what's next from there.

  • Pick one process to start, not the whole business
  • Keep working exactly as you do now until the new system is proven
  • Your existing records and paperwork move with you — nothing is thrown out
  • Expand to the next process only when you're ready, not on our timeline
Where most businesses start

Online ordering & booking

The single biggest time-sink we see — and usually the fastest to get live, often inside 3–4 weeks.

Close second

Inventory & stock tracking
