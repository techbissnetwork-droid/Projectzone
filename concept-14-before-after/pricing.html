Common questions

Pricing, answered honestly.

Project fees are one-time — you're paying for the build, not renting the software. Ongoing costs like hosting renewal, domain renewal, extra business email accounts, or an optional support retainer are separate, predictable, and entirely optional beyond what's already included.

The prices above cover the core build. Third-party costs — payment processor fees, premium plugins or licenses, stock content you choose to license, paid ad spend — and any scope added after kickoff are billed separately and always agreed with you before we build anything.
