SSL & Business Email

The two things every new visitor checks before they trust you.

A browser warning or a reply from a free @gmail.com address costs you credibility before you've said a word. We set up SSL certificates and professional business email as standard on every project — so your site reads as secured and your inbox reads as a real company, from the very first click.

What actually changes

The moment someone sees your address, or lands on your site, they've already formed an opinion.

Before: yourbusiness@gmail.com, and a browser warning nobody trusts. After: you@yourbusiness.com, a padlock in the address bar, and a first impression that's already working for you.
What's included

Six things that happen automatically the moment you go live.

SSL and business email aren't extras you configure later — they ship with every TECHBISS project. Here's exactly what that covers, in plain language.

01

Domain Validated (DV)

The baseline padlock. We confirm you own the domain and issue the certificate within minutes — enough to stop "Not Secure" warnings and encrypt every page on the site.

02

Organization Validated (OV)

A step up: we verify your registered business actually exists, not just the domain. Anyone can check the certificate and see your real company name attached to it.

03

Extended Validation (EV)

The deepest check available — legal, physical and operational existence, all confirmed. Best suited to finance, healthcare, or anywhere customers hand over sensitive information.

04

Custom Domain Addresses

Every teammate gets name@yourbusiness.com. We size the mailbox count to your team, and add more the moment you hire.

05

Spam & Security Filtering

Inbound filtering catches phishing and spam before it reaches anyone's inbox, with encryption in transit as standard, not an upgrade.

06

Mailbox Storage & Backup

Generous storage per mailbox with automatic backups running in the background, so a deleted email is never actually gone for good.

Why it matters

The first few seconds decide whether someone trusts you.

A visitor doesn't read your About page before deciding whether to trust you — they glance at the address bar, and at the email you replied from. Both get judged in about the time it takes to blink.

  • No "Not Secure" warning scaring off a visitor before they've read a word
  • Every form, login and checkout encrypted in transit, not just the homepage
  • A reply that lands from you@yourbusiness.com, not a free consumer address
  • One inbox your whole team can use without looking improvised
