About TECHBISS

Infrastructure for a planet-scale economy

Founded in 2009 as a systems-integration boutique, TECHBISS now operates as the connective infrastructure layer for 482 enterprise and government clients — a single accountable partner across cloud, AI, security, data and enterprise software. We hold the line between ambition and delivery: architecture reviewed by the same engineers who carry the pager.

Headquartered in New York with delivery hubs in London, Singapore, São Paulo and Warsaw, our 3,400+ engineers and architects operate in-region, in-language, and under local regulatory command — because global scale means nothing if it can't clear a national audit.
