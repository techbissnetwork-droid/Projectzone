    §05 / METHODOLOGY

    A six-step delivery spine

    The same sequence runs under every engagement — scaled up or down, never skipped.

    01

    Discover

    Map the current-state architecture, risk posture and business constraints before proposing anything.

    02

    Architect

    Design a target architecture and a sequenced roadmap that engineers can actually execute against.

    03

    Build

    Stand up the platform in iterative, independently-shippable increments with continuous validation.

    04

    Migrate

    Move workloads and data with rollback-safe checkpoints — never a single high-risk cutover event.

    05

    Launch

    Go live behind progressive rollout controls, with the delivery team on point for stabilization.

    06

    Scale

    Hand off to managed operations with the runbooks, SLOs and cost model to scale confidently.
