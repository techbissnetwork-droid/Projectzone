One partner · no handoffs

We took over the jobs nobody wanted to own.

TECHBISS exists because of a pattern we kept seeing: a business with a designer, a developer, a hosting reseller and an IT friend — and nobody responsible when the site went down on a Sunday.

Why we exist

Six suppliers, one broken website, no answers.

The first client we ever rescued had a domain registered in a former employee's personal email, hosting paid by an agency that had shut down, and an SSL certificate that expired on a Friday night. Nobody had done anything wrong exactly — the responsibility had just been split until it disappeared.

So we do the whole stack. Domain, hosting, SSL, email, the website, the apps, the listings, the support. One partner, one contact, one place where the buck stops. It is a less glamorous business model than "creative agency", and it is the one that actually keeps small businesses online.
