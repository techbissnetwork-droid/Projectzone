TECHBISS brand files
====================

The wordmark is Syne ExtraBold, converted to outlines — so these SVGs render
identically everywhere without needing the font installed.

  logo.svg          white wordmark, lime mark   — for dark backgrounds
  logo-ink.svg      black wordmark and mark     — for light backgrounds, print
  logo-lime.svg     lime wordmark, white mark   — for a loud, single-colour use
  mark.svg          the mark on its own, lime   — avatars, app icons, stamps
  mark-ink.svg      the mark on its own, black
  social.png        1200x630 — what people see when your link is shared
  icon-512.png      app icon
  icon-192.png      app icon
  icon-180.png      iPhone home screen
  icon-32.png       browser tab

  ../favicon.svg    the favicon the site uses

Colours
  Ink      #0b0b0d   the background everything sits on
  Lime     #c9ff3d   the one loud colour — use it sparingly
  Paper    #f4f4f6   text on dark
  Violet   #7b61ff   secondary accent
  Warm     #ff6b3d   used for warnings only

Type
  Syne ExtraBold     headings and the wordmark
  Manrope            body text
  Azeret Mono        small labels and numbers

Using them
  The site already uses these. To swap in your own, go to the admin area,
  Branding & SEO, and upload a logo, favicon or sharing image there — anything
  you upload replaces the file here without touching it.

  Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the mark. Do not
  stretch it, recolour the wordmark and mark separately, or put the lime version
  on a mid-tone background — it needs the contrast.
