Pricing questions.

The awkward ones, answered up front.

What isn't included in these prices?

Third-party running costs: domain registration (typically $12–20/year), hosting ($5–40/month depending on the plan), email licences (about $6/user/month for Workspace or 365), and any paid fonts, stock photos or plugins you choose. We set all of these up in your own accounts and you pay the provider directly — we take no cut.

How does payment work?

50% to start, 50% on go-live for build packages. Care plans are billed monthly in advance and can be cancelled any time with 30 days' notice. Custom projects over $4,500 are split into milestone payments tied to delivery stages written into the scope.

What if the project needs more than the package covers?

We tell you before starting, not halfway through. The written scope lists exactly what's included; anything outside it is quoted as an add-on and you approve it before any work happens. There are no surprise invoices at the end.

Can I pay monthly for the build instead?

For Business and Custom packages, yes — we can spread the build cost over 6 or 12 months alongside a care plan. Ownership of the code and accounts still transfers to you at launch, not at the end of the payment term.

Do you work with small budgets?

Often. If Starter is out of reach, tell us the number you have and what matters most. Sometimes the honest answer is that a properly configured Google Business Profile and a single-page site is all you need this year — and we'll say so rather than sell you a package.

What happens if we want to leave?

You take everything. Domain, hosting, email, code, content, analytics — all already in your name. We hand over documentation and, if you like, brief the next developer. No exit fee, no data held back.
