02 — Process

How a project goes live.

Four steps, the same order every time — nothing skipped to hit a date.

Step 01 · Day 0

Conversation, not a pitch

You describe what the business does and what's missing. We tell you what's worth doing first — and what isn't. No script, no deck, no pressure to buy the whole stack on day one.

Step 02 · Day 1–3

Written scope, fixed price

Everything in the build, listed on one page. One number. Third-party costs — domain registration, hosting, email licences — billed to you at cost, in your own name, with nothing marked up.

Step 03 · Build

Build and set up

Domain, hosting, SSL, email and the site itself, configured together as one system under your own accounts. You get the logins, documented, at handover — not a zip file and a goodbye.

Step 04 · Ongoing

Live, then watched

Launch, then monitoring, nightly backups, certificate and domain renewals, and support from the same people who built it. This is the part most vendors skip.
